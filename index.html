Privacy Policy

Morich AI Automation

Effective date: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Morich AI Automation provides business automation services, including workflows that process customer enquiries received through WhatsApp. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you communicate with our services.

2. Information We Process

Depending on your interaction with our services, we may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number or account identifier.
  • Messages you send, including enquiry and quotation requests.
  • Message identifiers, timestamps, message types, and delivery-related information.
  • Information you voluntarily provide about your project or business needs.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Receive and respond to customer enquiries.
  • Organize and qualify business leads.
  • Route enquiries to the appropriate team for follow-up.
  • Maintain service records, troubleshoot errors, and protect the system.
  • Operate and improve our business automation workflows.

4. WhatsApp and Service Providers

WhatsApp messages are processed through Meta's WhatsApp services. Depending on the workflow used, information may also be processed by our hosting, database, automation, or AI service providers to deliver the requested functionality. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Storage and Security

Information may be stored in our application database and related operational systems. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including customer follow-up, recordkeeping, security, and legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by record type. Information is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed, where appropriate and technically feasible.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate our services, with authorized personnel handling an enquiry, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your enquiry, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or legitimate business reasons.

9. Children's Privacy

Our business automation services are not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
